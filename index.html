THE LOGIC

Four checks before a rupee of MDR.

01

Who is paid?

P2P stays free. The new rule selects merchant payments only.

02

How is it funded?

Direct bank-account UPI is covered. Credit-linked UPI follows separate rules.

03

Which merchant?

P2PM micro merchants stay at zero. The acquirer controls the classification.

04

Which category?

Standard, industry programme and capital-market payments use different rates.

METHODOLOGY

What's certain. What's not.

Exact implementation: standard P2M has a strict “above ₹2,000” test. At ₹75,000 and above, ₹300 is the ceiling. P2PM status overrides the ticket threshold.

GST toggle: PIB says GST applies to MDR-like charges, not to the payment amount. NPCI's final FAQ does not spell out the invoice mechanics, so 18% is offered only as an estimate. Input tax credit is not modelled.

Education and settlement: the FAQ says education gets a flat or capped treatment but does not publish one universal number. It also does not change settlement cycles. Confirm both with the acquiring bank or PSP.

Headline correction

“UPI payments above ₹2,000 will be charged” is incomplete. Consumers pay nothing, and many merchant payments are exempt or separately treated.
